### New
- **Music in other rooms.** When nothing plays in the TV's room, Klangbild shows what's playing in another room instead, with that room's name next to the time. As soon as music starts again in the TV's room, it switches back. The other rooms are picked on the setup page.
- **Screen off when no one's there.** With presence sensors picked on the setup page, Klangbild turns the screen black a minute after everyone has left the room, and the music keeps playing. The picture comes back when someone walks in or presses a key. If the room stays empty, Klangbild turns off later, just as it does when no music plays.
- **Notifications.** Home Assistant can send short messages to the TV, like "Washer · Done", which show next to the time for a few seconds. Once Notifications is turned on in the settings, the TV appears in Home Assistant as a device to send them to, like a phone.
