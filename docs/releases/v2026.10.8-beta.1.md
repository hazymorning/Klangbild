### New
- Music from speakers in other rooms shows while nothing plays here, with the room's name next to the time. The rooms are chosen on the setup page, and "Music in other rooms" in the settings turns it off.
- With presence sensors chosen on the setup page, the screen goes dark while no one is in the room, and the music plays on. Someone coming in, or any key, brings it back. The setting is "Screen off" under Timer.
- Notifications from Home Assistant can show next to the time for a few seconds. Turned on under Information in the settings, the TV shows up in Home Assistant as a device to send them to, like a phone.
