### Better
- When a song has no lyrics, the Lyrics entry in the quick settings still says On, just quieter, so it no longer looks switched off.
- While a new way of showing the queue is being prepared, its name in the quick settings shimmers softly, so you can see it is on its way.

### Bug fixes
- The mirror images under the covers in Cover Flow no longer flicker or jump when the song changes.
- The light around the cover no longer jumps up when a speaker starts buffering again.
