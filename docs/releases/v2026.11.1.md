### Better
- Cover Flow shows one large cover beside the title while there's only one cover to show, like with a single album, a station or an audiobook. The covers come back side by side as soon as there's more than one.
- The songs of a station stay on one card in Cover Flow, whatever pictures they bring.
- Klangbild's icon on the TV's home screen shows the new logo.

### Bug fixes
- A song could keep the generated cover until the end when its own cover came late or didn't load the first time. Its cover now takes the generated one's place once it comes, so that should no longer happen.
- When a new song started, the previous song and its lyrics could stay on screen for a few seconds, more often in Cover Flow and with songs by several artists. That should no longer happen.
