### New
- **Music in other rooms.** Klangbild can now show what's playing in other rooms while nothing plays in the TV's room, with that room's name next to the time. To use it, pick those rooms' speakers on the setup page.
- **Screen off when no one's there.** With presence sensors from Home Assistant, Klangbild can now turn the screen black once everyone has left the room, while the music keeps playing. The picture comes back when someone walks in or presses a key. To use it, pick the sensors on the setup page.
- **Notifications.** Klangbild can now show notifications from Home Assistant, like "Washer · Done", next to the time for a few seconds. It's off by default. Once Notifications is turned on in the settings, the TV appears in Home Assistant as a device to send them to, like a phone.

### Better
- Play here has a new choice, While open, so the TV plays only while Klangbild is open and music from Music Assistant shouldn't start on it while another app is in front. Turning Play here on now picks it. Where Play here was already on, nothing changes.

### Bug fixes
- Turning on Play here from Home Assistant sometimes left the TV off as a speaker. That should no longer happen.
