### Better
- The space under a title grows with its size, so letters that reach below the line never crowd the artist.
- Switching between Cover Flow and the single cover is quicker. While the new view is being made, three dots pulse after the Queue value.
- The setup page says so when it can't reach the TV.
- Klangbild has been built with Kotlin Multiplatform since the last version, which its notes left out. That's why the app got larger.

### Bug fixes
- The remote's media keys no longer bring another app's player to the front. Stop pauses, and the skip keys change the track.
- Lyrics timed for another recording of a song are left out.
- A key pressed right after the start no longer resets the settings.
- When Home Assistant refuses the sign-in, Klangbild stops trying, since each try counts there as a failed login, and the setup screen asks to sign in again.
- Swapping the lights' sides on the setup page reaches the lights at once.
- On a player that can't seek, rewinding and fast forwarding no longer show a jump that never happens.
- A damaged settings file no longer keeps Klangbild from starting.
- The setup screen's QR code stays sharp on 720p screens.
- On the light background, the screen no longer darkens for a moment when the view changes.
- A few rare glitches in the stage's changes are gone, like a progress bar that showed full while it faded out.
