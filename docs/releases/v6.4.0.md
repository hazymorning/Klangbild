### New
- At a Music Assistant party, the quick settings can show the party's QR code where the cover stands. Guests scan it to add songs.
- Lights that are off can now stay off. One you switch on joins in.
- Lyrics can also be turned on in the settings.
- The quick settings now open all settings too.

### Better
- The settings are sorted into a few groups. Options that need something set up first are dimmed and say what.
- The window view is now called Landscape.
- Up next changes when you change the queue during a song.
- Holding a key moves through the settings evenly.
- Until you first open them, the screen shows that arrow down opens the quick settings.

### Bug fixes
- Up next no longer shows a song from the old queue after a new one starts.
