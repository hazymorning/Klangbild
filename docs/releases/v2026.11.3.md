### New
- **Artist info.** With Music Assistant, Klangbild can now show a few sentences about the artist beside the cover, where Music Assistant has them. A longer text turns its pages as the song plays, and when Lyrics is on, songs with lyrics still show their lyrics. To use it, turn on Artist info in the settings.
- **The cover edge to edge.** Klangbild can now show the cover as tall as the screen, starting right at its left edge, with the title in the space beside it. To use it, choose Edge to edge under Cover in the settings.

### Better
- The cover and the text beside it are a little larger.
- In Cover Flow, the covers and the text leave more room at the sides of the screen, and the middle cover is a little larger.
- The slow drift that protects OLED screens is now half as large, so the picture stays closer to the middle.

### Bug fixes
- In the TV's list of apps, the corners of Klangbild's icon could be cut off. That should no longer happen.
