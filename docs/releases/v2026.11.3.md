### New
- **Artist info** shows a few sentences about the artist next to the cover, split into pages that change as the song plays. The text comes from Music Assistant, so it only shows up for artists Music Assistant has a description for. It's off by default and can be turned on in the settings or the quick settings. Songs with lyrics still show their lyrics when Lyrics is on.
- **Edge to edge** is a new way to show the cover. It fills the whole height of the screen, right up to the left edge, with the title and everything else next to it. It's one of the choices for View in the quick settings.

### Better
- The quick settings have a new View entry for switching between the normal view, Edge to edge and Cover Flow. Queue now just turns the list of the next songs on or off.
- The cover is a little bigger, and the space to the edges of the screen is now the same on all four sides.
- In Cover Flow, there's more space between the covers and the sides of the screen, and the cover in the middle has grown a bit.
- The whole picture still drifts slowly to protect OLED screens from burn-in, but only half as far as before, so it stays closer to the center.

### Bug fixes
- Klangbild's icon has a new design that fills whatever shape the TV gives it. Before, some TVs cut off its corners in the list of apps.
