### New
- **Screen** is the new last entry in the quick settings while something is playing or paused. It turns the screen black while the music and the lights behind the TV keep going, and any key on the remote brings the picture back. Thanks for the idea in the [discussion](https://github.com/hazymorning/Klangbild/discussions/114).

### Better
- Track number can now also stand in the top line. Its setting offers Top and Bottom, and when the queue shows as a list beside the cover, the number always stands in the top line, since the list takes the place of the next song. Thanks to the [discussion](https://github.com/hazymorning/Klangbild/discussions/115) for pointing this out.
- Song changes and Cover Flow now need less work from the TV.

### Bug fixes
- With Artist info on, the old artist's text now fades out with the song when the next one starts. Before, it disappeared at once.
- When the music moved between this room's speaker and one in another room, the room's name in the top line could appear or disappear a moment too early. It now changes together with the song.
