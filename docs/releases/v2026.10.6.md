### New
- A new Speaker line under System in the settings switches between the speaker chosen on the phone and this TV, which then plays here.
- A new Update line under System checks for a new version and installs it. With Check for updates below it, Klangbild can also check by itself when it starts.
- Holding left or right on the remote seeks within a song.

### Better
- On the setup page, This TV shows up among the speakers as soon as Music Assistant is set up, and choosing it turns on Play here.
- The lyrics fade evenly around the line sung, and with the single cover their lines stand farther apart.
- The queue's list gives its tracks more room, with the song in the middle between the time and the list and no Up next line. Each small cover now frames its title and artist.
- On the dark wall, the title, the artist and the quieter lines stand further apart in brightness.
- A track without a cover shows part of the Klangbild mark in gloss on a dark sleeve, in place of the round sand plate. Its color follows the artist, so one artist's records match.

### Gone
- The paper landscape makes way for the coming visualizer, along with its settings. Up on the remote, which showed it, now opens the settings.

### Bug fixes
- When the same song is reported again, the text no longer dims for a moment.
