### New
- Klangbild now runs on Android TV 9 and later, not only on 12 and later.

### Bug fixes
- In Cover Flow, details that arrive a moment late, like the album's year, now fade in instead of jumping.
- When the speaker has been buffering, the light on the wall glides back instead of jumping.
- A change on the TV is no longer lost when your phone or Home Assistant changes the settings at the same moment.
- In the landscape, the lights behind the TV now follow a change in the weather as slowly as the picture does.
- Covers with see-through parts, like many station logos, no longer give the wall and the lights a dark cast.
