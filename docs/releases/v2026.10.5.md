### New
- Your TV can now be one of Music Assistant's speakers and play in time with the others. Turn on "Play here" under System in the settings, or tick the box on the setup page. It needs Music Assistant 2.10.7 or later.
- The TV shows up in Music Assistant under its own name and keeps playing while Klangbild is closed.
- When music starts on the TV, Klangbild shows it, and once it stops, Klangbild goes back to your speaker.
- On the setup page, "This TV" can be the speaker Klangbild follows.

### Better
- With Music Assistant, the progress bar and the lyrics follow the song to a fraction of a second.
- On the setup page, a short note now helps you pick the right speaker when it shows up twice.
